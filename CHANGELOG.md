## 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* `BrokenGlass` widget: snapshots any child and breaks it into shards.
* `ShatterPattern` with `fine`, `chunky` and `spiderWeb` presets.
* `GlassStyle` with `window`, `screen` and `frost` presets.
* `BrokenGlassController` for imperative `shatter()` / `restore()` / `toggle()`.
* `breakOnTap` for breaking at the touch point, and `scatter: false` for
  crack-in-place damage.
